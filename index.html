Community Guidelines

Keep EduMate Zone useful for everyone.

A focused community helps students find useful academic information without unnecessary noise.

✓
Keep conversations within reasonable limits.
✓
Keep discussions related to studies and academic activities.
✓
Use stickers minimally.
✓
Avoid irrelevant posts, messages and shares.
✓
Do not share unrelated group or channel links.
✓
Need something? Tag the relevant support/admin team.
✓
Respect other students and keep communication professional.
✓
Share EduMate Zone with classmates who may benefit.
